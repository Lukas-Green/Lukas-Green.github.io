# LUXIGA Brand — Market Research
**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Agent:** RADAR

## Executive Summary
Portland's tech job market remains soft — the metro lost 22,700 nonfarm jobs in the year to January 2026, with only a modest rebound projected for the rest of the year. However, the freelance/agency market nationally is expanding (86M U.S. freelancers projected by 2027), and web development retainer pricing is squarely in LUXIGA's $2,500–4,000/month target range. AI is reshaping agency pricing models: content costs are dropping 20–35% while strategy and technical work holds or increases — a direct opportunity for a design technologist positioning that leads with strategy.

## Market Trends

- **Source:** [Portland's Job Market Cools — Spreaker/Portland Business Journal](https://www.spreaker.com/episode/portland-s-job-market-cools-what-workers-need-to-know-in-2026--71413696)
- **Finding:** Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro lost 22,700 nonfarm jobs over the year ending January 2026 (–1.8%). Oregon shed 18,000+ net jobs in 2025. Nike cut ~1,400 roles in April 2026 (tech and global ops), Intel cut ~1,300 local roles in late 2024. CompTIA projects only slight tech growth for the remainder of 2026.
- **Impact:** medium
- **Action:** Avoid pitching to displaced large-enterprise workers or former Nike/Intel contractors expecting high day rates. Focus on SMB clients who still need agency support.

---

- **Source:** [The Freelance Skills Demand Index 2026 — Jobbers.io](https://www.jobbers.io/the-freelance-skills-demand-index-2026/)
- **Finding:** FlexJobs projects 86M U.S. freelancers by 2027, up from 72M in 2025. Freelance specialists earn 40–60% more than generalists. Design technologist roles active on ZipRecruiter at $22–$50/hr; 1,000+ postings available. Average hourly pay for the title as of August 2026: $33.01.
- **Impact:** high
- **Action:** GROWTH signal. Specialist framing (design technologist, not "web agency") commands premium rates and filters better-fit clients. Lean into the title explicitly in LUXIGA positioning and cold outreach.

---

- **Source:** [AI Agency Pricing Guide 2026 — Digital Agency Network](https://digitalagencynetwork.com/ai-agency-pricing/)
- **Finding:** AI has reduced content creation and reporting costs 20–35% at agencies using AI tooling, but strategy, technical, and conversion optimization pricing is stable or increasing. Industry is shifting from billable-hours to outcome-based models ($5,000/implemented workflow). AI is a design tool, not a replacement — agencies that thrive use AI to deliver faster, not cheaper.
- **Impact:** high
- **Action:** GROWTH signal. Reframe LUXIGA deliverables around outcomes and speed, not hours. Consider piloting one workflow-based engagement to test outcome pricing. Lead with "AI-augmented" capability in proposals.

---

- **Source:** [Web Design Pricing 2026 — Project Cost Estimator](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/web-design-pricing-guide-2026)
- **Finding:** Retainer rates for SMBs: $500–$2,500/month; enterprise: $3,000–$8,000/month. Webflow-specific retainers: $1,500–$5,000/month. Experienced freelancers charge $75–$150/hr; agency blended rates $100–$250/hr. Freelancers with 15+ years average $172,000 annually.
- **Impact:** medium
- **Action:** LUXIGA's $2,500–4,000/month target is market-accurate and positions well at the upper SMB / lower mid-market tier. No need to adjust pricing; use this data in proposals to validate rates.

# ScholarOS / Gradian — Market Research
**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Agent:** RADAR

## Executive Summary
EdTech venture funding is down 26% in H1 2026 vs. H1 2025, with capital concentrating in workforce training and AI integration — not student-facing consumer apps. However, gamification research is strengthening (a 2026 meta-analysis shows measurable gains in higher ed), PWA technology is at full capability parity with native apps (70% user preference), and the LTI 1.3 standard now has 75% higher-ed institutional adoption, opening a viable future integration path. The competitive field in student productivity is very crowded, with new social/gamified entrants (Focus Town, Zensi) signaling the niche is active but also contested.

## Market Trends

- **Source:** [EdTech Funding News 2026 — HolonIQ](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/1b-in-edtech-venture-capital-to-date-funding-falls-short-of-last-years-midpoint-asia-mena-buck-the-trend)
- **Finding:** EdTech VC totalled $1B in H1 2026, down 26% from $1.35B in H1 2025. Deal volume stable (investor appetite persists), but average deal size contracting. Workforce training captured 70%+ of Q1 2026 funding. Median round: $4.7M; average round: $18.1M (top-heavy distribution).
- **Impact:** medium
- **Action:** RISK signal. VC funding for individual student-facing consumer apps is compressed. ScholarOS should prioritize revenue and user retention metrics over growth for fundraising readiness. Bootstrap path remains more realistic near-term.

---

- **Source:** [Gamification in Higher Education: Meta-Analysis 2026 — SAGE Journals](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21582440261421375)
- **Finding:** 2026 peer-reviewed meta-analysis confirms gamification produces higher effect sizes in Business, Mathematics, and Engineering at the graduate level. Competitive mechanics outperform narrative-only. Offline delivery over 3–6 month durations shows strongest results. Interventions tied to learning objectives (not peripheral) yield measurable academic performance gains.
- **Impact:** high
- **Action:** GROWTH signal. Cite this meta-analysis in ScholarOS pitch deck and onboarding copy. Ensure game mechanics are tied to actual academic milestones (assignment completion, study session goals), not generic point systems.

---

- **Source:** [30 Gamification Statistics 2026 — Engageli](https://www.engageli.com/blog/game-based-learning-statistics)
- **Finding:** Game-based learning is now a mainstream instructional approach in K-8. Level Up Learning national survey confirms widespread institutional adoption. Deep learning and academic performance benefit most; engagement effects are smaller.
- **Impact:** medium
- **Action:** Positioning should emphasize academic performance outcomes over "fun" — the research supports this and it resonates better with the actual buyer (students who want grades, not entertainment).

---

- **Source:** [Student Productivity Apps 2026 — Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/education-productivity-may-2026-growth-guide)
- **Finding:** Focus Town launched as a social study app with viral UGC (hundreds of TikTok videos in first week). Zensi gaining traction on StudyTok. AI-powered study tools (ChatGPT) at 78% student adoption. Notion dominant for Gen Z student productivity (customization + aesthetic appeal).
- **Impact:** high
- **Action:** RISK signal. Focus Town's social/gamified launch is a direct competitive signal in ScholarOS's space. Monitor its App Store ratings and feature set closely. Consider whether a "study-with-others" social layer is on ScholarOS's roadmap.

---

- **Source:** [LTI 1.3 University Adoption — 1EdTech](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/lti)
- **Finding:** LTI 1.3 now at 75% higher education institutional adoption (April 2026). Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, D2L all support it. LTI 1.1 support dropped by 1EdTech in 2022; major platforms defaulting to 1.3 for new registrations. OAuth 2.0 + JWT authentication standard.
- **Impact:** medium
- **Action:** GROWTH signal. LTI 1.3 integration is now the standard path for university distribution. When ScholarOS reaches institutional-readiness, an LTI 1.3 launch package (Canvas integration first) unlocks the campus channel without requiring custom contracts.

---

- **Source:** [PWA in 2026 — DEV Community](https://dev.to/riteshkokam/pwa-in-2026-why-progressive-web-apps-still-matter-1hn9)
- **Finding:** 70% of users prefer PWAs over downloading native apps. Full feature support in Chrome, Safari, Edge: push notifications, background sync, biometric auth, offline-first, file system access. PWA use case for native narrowed but native not replaced. Real adoption lifting engagement 30%+ in multiple platforms post-PWA cutover.
- **Impact:** high
- **Action:** GROWTH signal. ScholarOS's PWA architecture is well-timed — the platform is at full capability parity now. Lean into PWA in marketing copy: "No download required" and offline study mode are differentiators vs. native-app competitors.
