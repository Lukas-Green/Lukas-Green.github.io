# Bid / No-Bid Rubric

Ten dimensions, 1–5 each, 50 points max. Same structure as the Partnership
Intelligence rubric, retuned for public procurement.

Scored by the `/bid-triage` skill. Revise the anchors as real solicitations
prove them wrong — a rubric that has never been edited has never been used.

| # | Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Capability fit** | Adjacent at best; we'd be learning on their budget | Core to one service line, gaps in the rest | Squarely what we do, with work to show for it |
| 2 | **Set-aside eligibility** | Open formal procurement, no advantage | Certified firms invited among others | Direct award or reserved competition |
| 3 | **Value vs. capacity** | Too large to deliver, or too small to bother | Deliverable with strain | Right-sized for the bench we actually have |
| 4 | **Past-performance requirement** | Requires references we cannot produce | Requires some, we can stretch | None required, or ours exceed it |
| 5 | **Incumbent** | Entrenched incumbent, recompete is theatre | Incumbent present but scope changed | No incumbent, or incumbent underperforming |
| 6 | **Response effort** | Weeks of original writing, heavy forms | Several days, some reusable content | Days at most, mostly boilerplate |
| 7 | **Margin** | Below cost once response effort is counted | Acceptable | Strong, with follow-on work implied |
| 8 | **Agency relationship** | Never spoken to them | Some contact, no champion | Known buyer, prior conversation, warm |
| 9 | **Strategic value** | One-off, teaches us nothing | Useful reference | Opens an agency or a repeatable service line |
| 10 | **Deadline feasibility** | Tight enough that quality suffers | Workable with focus | Comfortable |

## Thresholds

- **38–50** Pursue
- **28–37** Watch
- **Under 28** Pass

## Automatic disqualifiers

Any one of these ends the evaluation regardless of score:

- Under 7 days to deadline with original writing required
- Licensed architect or engineer of record required
- Bonding required (public improvement, not services)
- Bound insurance required at bid rather than at award, with no funding for it
- Mandatory pre-bid meeting already passed
- Minimum past-performance count exceeds the ledger
- Scope centre of gravity outside capability

## Scoring discipline

Score from the solicitation text, not from what the work could become.
On ties in **Capability fit** or **Response effort**, take the lower score.
