Is a Bachelor of Science the right fit?

Before you change anything, here is how a BS differs from your BA and what carries over. Most of your credits come with you.

Current programGraphic Design, BA
Catalog year2024-25
Progress118 of 180 credits
Most of your credits carry over62 to go

Your BA now

  • Requires two years of a college-level second language
  • Graphic Design major requirements
  • General education, University Writing, Race and Ethnic Studies

Your BS after

  • No two-year language requirement (the Second Language Admission requirement, SLAD, can still apply if not yet met)
  • BS degree-type requirements (science and quantitative credits)
  • The same general education and writing credits carry over

What carries over: your general requirements, your Race and Ethnic Studies and University Writing credits, and your 100 and 200 level design courses. What changes is the degree-type requirements and any new major requirements.

What changes if you switch

Here is how your remaining requirements change moving from the BA to the BS.

No longer needed

  • The two-year second language requirement drops on the BS

Now needed

  • BS degree-type requirements (science and quantitative credits)
  • Any new major requirements for the BS track

Bottom line

Switching to the BS removes the two-year language requirement and adds the BS degree-type and major requirements. This is an estimate. An adviser confirms it, and your degree audit updates after the change is approved.
