The thinking behind this hub

  • Blockers first. The single most common registration failure is a hold a student discovers only when they try to sign up. This hub surfaces it at the top, in plain language, with the one action that clears it.
  • Tasks, not systems. Students think in verbs ("register," "pay," "change my major"), not in the names of the back-end systems. The hub is organized the way a student would ask, not the way the software is built.
  • Mobile-first, by default. Returning and working adults do a lot of this on a phone. Every task is designed to finish on a small screen, which is where the current experience breaks down.
  • One clear next step. The hub always answers "what now?" so no one gets bounced between offices or told to "look on the website."
  • Personal, from the student's own record. Credits, catalog year, holds, and deadlines are the student's own, so the page is about their path, not a generic wall of links.
